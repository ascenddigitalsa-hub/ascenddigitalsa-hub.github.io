01 About the resort

A place to pause, play and celebrate

Leisure beneath the palms.

Florence Palms Resort & Spa is a leisure and hospitality destination in Mamaneng, near Marble Hall. Guests visit for family days, swimming, accommodation, group gatherings, photography and special occasions.

Facility details, room rates, opening hours and availability are confirmed directly with resort management.
